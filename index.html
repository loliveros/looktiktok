lookuma_tiktok_test

lookuma_tiktok_test is a small personal application that connects to the owner's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and Display API, in order to retrieve and review the account's public profile information and list of recent videos.

What it does

  • Lets the account owner sign in with TikTok and grant explicit permission.
  • Reads basic profile information (user.info.basic).
  • Reads the owner's list of videos and their basic metadata (video.list).

What it does not do

  • It does not publish, edit or delete content on TikTok.
  • It does not sell, rent or share data with third parties.
  • It is not a public service: it is currently used for development and testing.
