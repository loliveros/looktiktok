Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains what information lookuma_tiktok_test (the "Service") accesses when you authorize it through TikTok, how it is used, and how you can control it.

1. Information we access

Only after you grant permission on TikTok's consent screen, the Service may access:

  • Basic profile information (scope user.info.basic): TikTok open ID, display name and avatar.
  • Video information (scope video.list): the list of your videos and their basic metadata, such as ID, title, description, duration, cover image, share link and public counts (views, likes, comments, shares).

The Service does not access your TikTok password, private messages, contacts or any data outside the scopes listed above.

2. How we use the information

The information is used only to show you your own profile and videos within the Service. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

3. Storage

The Service stores the access token and refresh token issued by TikTok, together with your TikTok open ID, so it can keep querying your account without asking you to sign in each time. Tokens are kept on the Service's own server and are never exposed publicly. Video metadata is requested from TikTok when needed.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties. Data is exchanged only with TikTok's official APIs.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while your authorization is active. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services), and you can ask us to delete any stored data by writing to laos.788@gmail.com. We will respond and delete it within a reasonable time.

6. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect stored tokens and data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest revision.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: laos.788@gmail.com.
